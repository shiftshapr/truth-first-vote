Why now

Neither party is going to make that choice for us. People inside both parties who put truth first can.

The starting platform

Truth First is global. Every Truth Country puts its own country first and also values every other people. America is the first Truth Country, in a confederation of interdependence. These pillars are a first draft, and the founding players will rewrite them.

Truth first

Every claim we make is sourced, scored and open to challenge, including this page.

Against corruption and drift

Government answers to people, not donors or lobbies. No more agencies, wars and programs that outlive their purpose because nobody is accountable for ending them.

Against surveillance

No mass monitoring of citizens, whether by the state or by platforms. Your life is not a data feed.

Against control over individuals

A government that protects your freedom instead of managing your life.

For human nature

Policy that fits how people actually live: with family, community and the land.

AI for the many

Use AI to close the gaps in distribution and fairness that we couldn't close before, and to protect people, not to rank them.

Country first, humanity whole

Each Truth Country is strong and sovereign, and none of them looks away when people anywhere are being destroyed.

The layered web

A layer on top of every web page where anyone can see the sources, challenge a claim and coordinate with others, without asking the platform's permission. It's the baseline everything else here is built on.
